# 天空城堡背景

使用内置 image_gen 工具生成，作为真实 Qt 界面的背景素材，不作为整张界面图片。原有首页模块与三页导航保持不变。

文件：`sky-palace.png`。参考风格：用户提供的浅蓝天空、城堡、轻透明卡片示例。实际界面卡片、按钮、文字和图标均由程序绘制。

最终生成提示：

Create a polished illustrated background asset for a Chinese racing voice assistant desktop app, landscape wide 16:9. Pastel airy anime watercolor aesthetic: luminous azure blue sky, soft fluffy white clouds, a delicate white fantasy European water-city palace with spires placed in the upper RIGHT third, subtle swirling thin pale-blue orbital ribbons around its towers. Top 24 percent is scenic sky and architecture. The central and lower main content area (right 83 percent below the top banner) must be very pale icy blue and white with extremely low detail and generous clean space, because real readable UI cards will be placed on it by code. The LEFT 15 percent is a pale translucent-looking sky margin with a very small cute original white water-droplet mascot with light blue fins at its bottom, gently sketched, playful and understated. Bottom RIGHT corner has very subtle blue floral petals and ribbon details. Soft mint and lavender secondary accents, gentle blue gradients, delicate watercolor texture, crisp high quality, elegant rather than busy. This is ONLY a BACKGROUND ILLUSTRATION, not an app mockup: absolutely NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO logos, NO UI cards, NO buttons, NO icon grids, NO gauges, NO borders. The image should work as a calm backdrop to a real white and pastel-blue application interface.
